Privacy Policy

Draft — last updated 18 August 2026
Important: This is a starting draft, not reviewed by a lawyer. Before collecting real user data, this should be reviewed against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) requirements by a qualified advisor.

1. What we collect

When you use Kaamgar, we may collect: your name, phone number, city/location, job descriptions you submit, and, for workers, trade and identity verification details collected in person at a Kaamgar office.

2. How we use it

We use this information to match customers with workers, scope and price jobs, verify worker identity, and communicate with you about bookings.

3. Worker verification data

Identity and skill documents collected during in-person worker verification are stored securely and used only for verification and platform trust purposes.

4. Who we share it with

Your name and phone number may be shared between a customer and worker once a booking is confirmed, so the job can be coordinated directly. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

5. Data storage

Data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database provider, with access-controlled security rules restricting who can read or write each type of data.

6. Your rights

You can request to see, correct, or delete the personal data Kaamgar holds about you by contacting us through the details on the website.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the platform develops. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact details listed on the Kaamgar website.
